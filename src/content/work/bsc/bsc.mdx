Best Shot is a patient's companion through fertility treatment — medication management and education, a streamlined pharmacy ordering experience, and expert-led micro-communities. It was sold to fertility clinics as a SaaS product.

I led front-end development, introducing NGRX for predictable state across a complex medical workflow, plus a front-end testing strategy and CI/CD pipelines.

I also built the app's mapping layer on Mapbox GL: a custom-designed map style, pharmacy search driven by device geolocation with distance filtering and reverse geocoding, and a deliberate switch between interactive maps and static tiles to keep rendering cost down where interactivity wasn't needed.
