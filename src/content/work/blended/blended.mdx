Blended helps divorced and separated parents handle money together. It imports transactions automatically, itemizes expenses against the terms of a divorce agreement, generates invoices, and settles balances through integrated payments — replacing the spreadsheets and text threads that usually fill that role.

As founding engineer I built the product end to end: the React and Ionic mobile app, the Firebase backend, and integrations with Plaid for account linking and Venmo for reimbursement. The first release shipped about four months after we started.

Blended launched in March 2025 and is used by 1,000+ parents.
