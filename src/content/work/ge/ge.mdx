GE Renewable Energy monitored a global fleet of wind, hydro, and solar assets through a patchwork of aging systems. Lifespan replaced them with a single multi-tenant platform for visualizing fleet-scale operational data, inspecting turbines for damage, planning maintenance cycles, and generating business intelligence reports.

I spent about sixteen months on the platform, working primarily on turbine inspections and the reporting module. I built a library of 22 report widgets on Highcharts — wind roses, production graphs, availability and work-order views — and standardized how every widget handled empty states and numeric precision. I also worked across the shared component library and maintained the reporting experience in English, German, and Brazilian Portuguese.

Lifespan launched in August 2022 and now serves over 2,000 users, recapturing an estimated $3 million a year in overhead.
