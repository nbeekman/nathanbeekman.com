After merging three major telecom brands, Charter needed one design system that could serve dozens of product teams building on different frameworks. Kite is that system — components, design tokens, patterns, and governance for design, accessibility, and brand.

The interesting constraint was framework diversity. Rather than maintaining parallel libraries, Kite is built as a single Stencil source compiled out to Angular, React, and Vue through framework output targets: 35 web components from one implementation. Accessibility was treated as a first-class concern with its own test suite rather than an afterthought.

Kite now supports more than 40 teams across 13 customer portals serving over 31 million Spectrum customers.
