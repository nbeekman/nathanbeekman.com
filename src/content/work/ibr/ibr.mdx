Comfort Zone helps organizations identify the factors impeding their employees. It automates the collection and organization of workforce data, then surfaces metrics that help administrators spot risk, deploy resources efficiently, and measure the results.

Built in Angular, with D3 visualizations powering the metrics behind those decisions.
